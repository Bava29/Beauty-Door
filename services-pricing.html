Before You Book

A little preparation makes your home beauty experience even smoother. Here's what to know before your Beauty Door visit.

Book Your Service

Choose a comfortable space at home with enough room for your selected service. We recommend keeping the area clean and ready before your beautician arrives.

Your beautician will arrive with the required tools and products for your selected service and guide you through the experience with care.

We maintain thoughtful hygiene practices throughout every visit and use carefully selected beauty products for a comfortable service experience.

You can select your preferred service, date and time while booking. If your plans change, please update or cancel your visit as early as possible.
